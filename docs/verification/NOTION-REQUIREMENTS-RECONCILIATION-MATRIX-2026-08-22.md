## Decision and scope boundary

This record answers one question only: whether a requirement or statement found in the linked Notion material is already implemented by MIAYAAR, absent, incompatible, or not sufficiently governed to treat as implementation work. It makes **no product, methodology, contract, data, API, or configuration change**. In particular, no Notion-era formula, weight, coefficient, threshold, fallback, score, property-applicability cell, or external data source is adopted through this document.

The owner-supplied clarification is controlling for this exercise: methodology v1.2, Core Types/contracts, the evidence pipeline, reproducibility, and historical data remain preserved; a substantive item without an approved governing source remains unresolved rather than inferred.[8] The repository’s frozen methodology therefore takes precedence over the historical AQAR v1.0 draft when the two differ.[3] [9]

## Reading the matrix

The **Missing** column identifies only a documented gap; it never authorises its implementation. `None — not a current implementation obligation` means the source is historical, strategic, unavailable, or outside the accepted MIAYAAR boundary. `UNRESOLVED / GOVERNANCE REVIEW`, `CONFLICT / GOVERNANCE REVIEW`, `SOURCE-GAP / GOVERNANCE REVIEW`, and `PLANNED / GOVERNANCE REVIEW` require a separate owner-approved scope before any technical work. `NOT ADOPTED` means that no MIAYAAR work should be derived from the referenced Notion statement.

## Reconciliation conclusion

The repository already implements the core evidence-led valuation boundary, DLD evidence pipeline, server-side public valuation path, rate limiting, four governed approaches under methodology v1.2, deterministic comparable selection, DLD-only market benchmarks, facts-only Evidence Integrity, traceability, CI/deployment evidence, and a read-only administrative governance surface. These results do **not** mean that Notion v1.0 is merged into the codebase; they show only where a current MIAYAAR capability independently aligns with a non-conflicting high-level reference.

The material requiring a future governance decision is narrow and explicit: the Property Type × Method × Field Applicability matrix; any forensic taxonomy or diagnostic decision rule; a temporal-backtesting/validation-claim protocol; the full Calibration Studio design; any formal review-state lifecycle; recurring research/publication processes; and any field compatibility mapping. The historical AQAR v1.0 weights, coefficients, adjustment layers, confidence heuristic, comparable hierarchy, composite indices, synthetic fields, and historical performance results are **not implementation gaps**. They remain historical references or conflicts pending an explicit owner decision.
